## Purpose

Define the MEGA Assistant MVP as a portfolio governance and traceability assistant, not an operational automation until sources and owners are confirmed.

## Procedure

1. Capture MEGA scope, business owner, platform owner, data owner and support owner.
2. Map milestones, dependencies, risks, CPS and R&R.
3. Link design evidence to source documents and platform modules.
4. Identify which parts are Existing, POC, Design or To build.
5. Return a product brief with build backlog, open decisions and approval gates.

## Quality gates

- Mark unverified source areas as To build.
- Do not present MEGA as live until source artifacts and MVP acceptance criteria exist.
- Any shared skill created from MEGA design requires steward approval.
